# SGI Shift Log — Installation Guide

## What is this?
A free app that runs on any phone. No app store needed.
You install it directly from a link in 3 taps.

---

## Install on Android (Samsung, Google Pixel, etc.)

1. Open **Google Chrome** on your phone
2. Go to: **[YOUR LINK HERE]**
3. Tap the **three dots (⋮)** in the top right corner
4. Tap **"Add to Home screen"**
5. Tap **"Add"**
6. The app icon will appear on your home screen

---

## Install on iPhone (iOS)

1. Open **Safari** (must use Safari, not Chrome)
2. Go to: **[YOUR LINK HERE]**
3. Tap the **Share button** (box with arrow pointing up) at the bottom
4. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Tap **"Add"** in the top right
6. The app icon will appear on your home screen

---

## First Time Setup

When you first open the app:
1. Enter your **full name**
2. Enter your **assignment location** (it may be pre-filled)
3. Enter your **supervisor's name** (optional)
4. Tap **Get Started**

---

## Logging a Shift

1. Tap **+ Add** in the top right
2. Fill in the date, start time, and end time
3. Check all duties you performed
4. Note any incidents (or type "None")
5. Tap **Save Entry**

---

## Sharing Your Report

1. Tap the **Report** tab at the bottom
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Tap **Share / Export Report** to send via text, email, or WhatsApp
4. Tap **Print / Save as PDF** to save a printable copy

---

## Your Data

- All data is stored **on your phone only**
- Nothing is shared automatically
- Go to **Settings → Export all data** to back up your entries
- Works offline — no internet required after install
